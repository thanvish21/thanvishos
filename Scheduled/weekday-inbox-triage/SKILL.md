---
name: weekday-inbox-triage
description: Triage unread emails each weekday morning by urgency, provide 1-line summaries, and draft replies for urgent items.
---

Triage unread inbox emails for the morning:

1. Retrieve all unread emails from the inbox using the connected email tools.
2. Group the emails into urgency tiers:
   - **Urgent / Needs Response Today**: Direct requests, deadlines, action items, or critical client/team messages.
   - **Important / Can Wait**: Items that need attention soon but not immediately today.
   - **Informational / Low Priority**: Newsletters, automated notifications, status updates, receipts.
3. For each email, provide:
   - Sender and subject
   - A single-line concise summary of the core message or request
4. For all emails categorized as needing a response today:
   - Draft a complete, context-aware, and professional reply for review.
5. Present the final triage in a clear, well-structured format.
